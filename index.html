Supply Chain  ·  Fulfillment

Digital Proof of Delivery

Eliminated delivery leakage through real-time digital verification across 3 geographies and 6K+ vendors.

Problem

Delivery verification was entirely manual across 3 geographies. Wrong supplies went undetected for weeks — no audit trail meant disputes resolved retroactively, leaking ₹2.6Cr/year.

Strategic Decision

Verification had to happen at the moment of delivery — not after. We shifted the control point from finance reconciliation to physical handoff, closing the loop before errors entered the system.

What I Built
  • Real-time photo verification and signature capture at point of delivery
  • Automated mismatch detection with vendor reconciliation triggers
  • Integration with order management and vendor tracking systems
  • Escalation workflows replacing retroactive dispute resolution

Operating Philosophy

How I Build

Three principles that show up in every product decision I make.

01

Systems Over Features

I design architecture that reduces downstream complexity. Before writing a spec, I map how the whole system behaves — data flows, failure modes, integration surfaces. Every feature ships as part of a coherent architecture, not a standalone addition.

02

Business Model First

Revenue, cost structure, and margin implications drive prioritization — not user story velocity. Before touching UI, I understand the unit economics and operational constraints. Product decisions follow business logic, not the reverse.

03

Integration Is Strategy

Platform decisions compound over time. Every API contract, every ERP sync, every vendor data model is a long-term bet. I treat integration architecture as a strategic decision — not a technical detail left to engineers.

About

At Livspace, this approach has delivered across India, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East — building platforms that handle vendor ecosystems, financial reconciliation, field governance, and retail tech from scratch.
